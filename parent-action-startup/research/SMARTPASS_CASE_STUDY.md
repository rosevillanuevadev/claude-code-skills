# SmartPass — Strategic Case Study Seed

SmartPass is not a direct competitor. It is a case study in building a narrow school workflow product.

## Starting facts from prior research
These must be re-verified before external use:
- SmartPass focused on digital hall passes / student movement workflows
- it grew to thousands of schools
- it was acquired by Raptor Technologies in 2024
- purchase price was not publicly disclosed in the prior research

## Why it matters
The strategic lesson is not “copy SmartPass.” It is:
- a seemingly small school workflow can be valuable
- narrow tools can become infrastructure if the workflow is frequent and painful
- the buyer can value visibility, control, and auditability more than the surface-level task suggests

## Research questions
- What did V1 actually do?
- Who was the buyer?
- What operational pain justified budget?
- What did SmartPass deliberately not do initially?
- How did product scope expand?
- What made it strategically valuable to Raptor?
