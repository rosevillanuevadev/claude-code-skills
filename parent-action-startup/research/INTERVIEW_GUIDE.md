# Interview Guide

## Busy parent
- Walk me through everything school sent you last week.
- Which child was each item for?
- How did you know whether you needed to act?
- Where do you keep due dates or forms?
- What happens when you are busy or traveling?
- Do you delegate any school tasks to a spouse, assistant, grandparent, or caregiver?
- Tell me about the last absence. How did you notify the school? Was another formal excuse required?
- What school task do you repeatedly wish someone else would just handle?
- What do you use today: diary, email, Teams, Google Classroom, chat, portal, screenshots, paper?
- What do you still miss despite those tools?

Avoid asking “Would you pay for this?” too early. First quantify current cost in time, missed actions, stress, and delegated labor.

## School administrator / teacher
- How do parents report absences today?
- What makes an absence formally excused?
- How are excuse records stored?
- How do you send formal notices?
- Which notices require acknowledgment, consent, or documents?
- How do you know who has not responded?
- Who follows up?
- What gets tracked in spreadsheets?
- Which parent workflows still use paper?
- What would prevent you from accepting a parent-originated digital record?
- What systems must remain unchanged?
