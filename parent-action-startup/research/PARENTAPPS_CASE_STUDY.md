# Parentapps — Forensic Case Study Seed

Parentapps is strategically important because its origin story resembles the problem being explored: parents frustrated by school letters and fragmented parent-school communication.

## Starting facts from prior research

These facts were previously researched during ideation and must be re-verified with primary or high-quality sources before external use:
- founded around 2015
- founders reportedly Kevin Clayton and Hailey Clayton
- origin story involved frustration with school letters reaching parents through children/school bags
- hundreds of schools reportedly adopted within the first few years
- investment from notable UK business figures was reported
- company was acquired by Community Brands UK in 2021
- later marketing claims 1,000+ schools / international reach
- historical pricing documents showed annual school pricing in the hundreds to low-thousands of GBP depending package

## Questions Claude should answer forensically

### Founding
- What exact problem did founders describe?
- What did the first product do?
- Was it mobile-first, white-label, school-branded, or web-first?
- Did parents pay anything?

### Go-to-market
- How were the first 10, 50, 100 schools acquired?
- Was sales founder-led?
- Did they use local school relationships?
- What was the sales cycle?
- Was implementation/service work important?

### Product evolution
- What features existed at launch?
- When were payments, bookings, forms, websites, MIS integrations, etc. added?
- Which features appeared before vs after scale?

### Economics
- Historical prices
- setup fees
- add-ons
- any public revenue/headcount estimates
- any Companies House filings that can illuminate scale, if legally/publicly accessible

### Acquisition
- acquisition date
- acquirer rationale
- whether financial terms were disclosed
- product position inside Community Brands after acquisition

### Lessons
Separate lessons that are still valid from those that depended on launching in 2015.

Do not conclude “therefore our product will succeed.” Use Parentapps as evidence that the pain can support a business, not proof of current whitespace.
