# Validation Plan

The goal is to identify the smallest workflow that creates enough value to pay for.

## Parent validation
Prefer observation over opinion.

Ask parents to show the last 2–4 weeks of school communications and reconstruct:
- where each item arrived
- what required action
- what was forgotten or delayed
- what was delegated
- what had to be copied into another system
- what was still unresolved

Key question:
> Show me the last school thing you almost missed or had to chase.

Also test:
- absence reporting
- excuse-letter process
- multiple children
- spouse/assistant/grandparent delegation
- paper diary dependency

## School-side validation
Ask staff to show the last parent-related process that required follow-up.

Examples:
- absence excuses
- permission slips
- acknowledgments
- requested documents

Key question:
> Show me how you know which parents still owe you something.

## Prototype tests
Test three prototypes separately:
1. Needs Action inbox
2. Report absence / send excuse
3. No-account teacher acknowledgment link

Measure confusion and completion time. Do not pitch unrelated roadmap features.

## Kill/modify signals
Reconsider the concept if:
- parents will not enter or forward information and extraction cannot solve it economically
- schools refuse third-party parent-originated records categorically
- existing products already solve the target workflow with lower friction and price
- the product requires integrations before delivering value
- support burden from school-specific policies overwhelms the lightweight model
