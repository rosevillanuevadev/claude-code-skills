# Business Model Hypotheses

Nothing here is final pricing.

## Customer possibilities

### Parent-paid
A parent subscribes directly. School adoption is not required.
Potential paid value:
- multiple children
- delegation to spouse/assistant/caregiver
- notice extraction
- larger history/attachment storage
- email forwarding
- advanced reminders

### School-paid
School pays annually; parents use the parent experience without charge.
Potential paid value:
- bulk notices
- completion tracking
- outstanding parent dashboard
- reminders
- CSV roster import
- audit/history
- school roles

### Hybrid
Parent-first product creates bottom-up usage. Schools that see repeated incoming records can claim a workspace. If a school licenses, parent access for that school can become included.

## Product-led school acquisition hypothesis

A parent sends an absence/excuse from the app to a teacher.
The teacher receives a polished email and can acknowledge through a secure link without an account.
The teacher experiences the product before procurement.
Repeated use may create a natural path to school adoption.

This is a hypothesis to validate, not an excuse to add viral mechanics prematurely.

## Pricing research

Do not copy competitor prices mechanically. Research:
- what small schools already pay for parent communication/forms
- whether the buyer is principal, admin, operations, communications, registrar, or owner
- implementation fees vs self-serve
- parent willingness to pay for school-only organization
- whether school pricing by enrollment creates unnecessary friction

## Core commercial question

Can this product replace enough recurring coordination/follow-up work that a parent or school willingly pays every year?
