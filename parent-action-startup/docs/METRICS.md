# Metrics

Avoid vanity metrics such as time spent in app.

## Parent product
- % of new users who add first child
- % who create first school action
- % who complete first absence/excuse send
- weekly active families with at least one meaningful action
- % of action items completed before due date
- repeat use across 4+ weeks
- number of manual school-coordination steps replaced
- delegation usage

## School/recipient loop
- delivery success
- recipient open rate
- acknowledgment rate
- median time from parent send to school acknowledgment
- % of school recipients who interact without account
- repeated recipient schools/domains

## School workspace later
- notices sent
- completion rate
- outstanding actions per notice
- reminder effectiveness
- staff time saved (survey/interview)
- renewal rate

## North-star candidate

A candidate metric is:
> Completed parent-school actions per active family per month.

Do not finalize a north-star metric until real usage exists.
